01 / ABOUT ME

Problem solver.
Product builder.

A little about the developer behind the code.

I’m Shakir, a full stack developer from Jamalpur, Bangladesh, building fast, user-focused web applications from interface to database.

Open to opportunities

I work across the whole stack: crafting responsive interfaces with React and Next.js, designing REST APIs with Node.js and Express, and modelling data in MongoDB. I care about clean structure, performance and the small details that make a product feel right.

I like turning a rough idea into a working product: breaking down the problem, choosing a sensible architecture and refining it through feedback. Photography and photo/video editing are my creative outlets, and they sharpen my eye for layout and visual detail.

My focus is on building real-world products, such as BazarDor, a Bengali price-tracking app, and on integrating AI to make digital experiences more helpful.
